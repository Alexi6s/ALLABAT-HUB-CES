# Roles and permissions

_The tables are generated by `npm run docs:api` from `apps/api/src/policy.ts`. Do not edit by hand._

Access is **deny by default**. Every protected route declares the permission it needs; the check runs before the request body is read, so an unauthorised caller learns nothing about the record. Record-level rules (you can only see your own ventures, applications, documents) are enforced in each handler and return **404** for records that belong to someone else.

## Three separate things

1. **Account category**: what you registered as (student, lecturer, alumni, external_entrepreneur, external_participant, partner, mentor_applicant, or staff). It is a claim until verified.
2. **Verification status**: unverified, pending, under review, more info required, verified, rejected. Institutional features need `verified` plus a confirmed email.
3. **Roles**: what the platform lets you do. Roles are never self-assigned (see rules below).

## Governance rules (each one has an automated test)

- **Roles come only from governance.** Verification approval grants the category role. Mentor review grants `mentor`. Administrators grant only staff roles (programme_coordinator, funding_officer, incubation_manager, support_officer, admin, super_admin). Granting or revoking admin or super_admin needs a super administrator. Nobody can change their own roles, and the last super administrator cannot be removed.
- **Changing affiliation resets trust.** Editing institutional details sets verification back to unverified and removes the category role until a reviewer approves again.
- **Separation of duties.** You cannot decide your own verification, review your own mentor application, decide your own programme application, or review a venture, review a milestone, evaluate funding, or disburse funds for a venture you belong to. The person who disburses a grant must differ from the person who approved it; even a super administrator cannot pay their own approval. Disbursement needs a signed agreement and `funding.disburse`.
- **Account status.** A suspended user may sign in but can only reach their account and the support routes. Disabled and closed users cannot sign in. Any status change deletes all of that user's sessions.
- **Evidence is labelled.** Milestone evidence is `self_reported` until a reviewer marks it `verified`; reports count only the latter as verified.
- **Mentor governance.** Mentors become visible only after the configured number of distinct reviewer approvals; reviewers can recuse; a mentor can be suspended; a mentor cannot match with themselves.
- **Database row-level security is not used.** SQLite has none. Enforcement is in the application layer and is covered by the all-routes authentication and permission test (`apps/api/test/security.test.ts`).
